---
section: Behaviour changes in all modes
---

- GoBGP mode: a BFD Down no longer resets a BGP session at once while the
  session is coming up (Connect to OpenConfirm, or Established in the
  same or the next second of its uptime). A route reflector with
  `update-source` re-keys its multihop BFD session then, and BOOTy reset
  the RR session at the start now and then. BOOTy checks again and resets
  the session if BFD is not Up within multiplier x 1 s (#849).
