---
section: Behaviour changes in all modes
---

- DHCP mode encodes and decodes DHCPv4 and DHCPv6 messages with
  `github.com/insomniacslk/dhcp` (BSD-3-Clause, through the fork
  `MaxRink/dhcp`, see `docs/dependency-forks.md`); the clients and the
  bytes they send do not change. Replies are read like networkd 255: a
  DHCPv4 option that comes twice is concatenated (RFC 3396), a DHCPv4
  option whose length does not fit its type and a DHCPv6 DNS option
  whose length is not a multiple of 16 are ignored, a truncated DHCPv6
  option drops the message, IA or address, and a search list (option
  119) with chained compression pointers is read (#835).
