---
section: Upgrade notes for CAPRF
---

- New opt-in vars key `IMAGE_COSIGN_REQUIRE_OCI` (default `false`, YAML
  `provision.image.cosignRequireOCI`). With a cosign policy set, an image or
  sysext source that is not `oci://` fails `verify-image` (status
  `BOOTY-IMG-001`) and dry-run before any disk is touched, instead of being accepted with
  its checksum. Like the other cosign options, a standby payload or deploy
  archive can not change it (#955).
