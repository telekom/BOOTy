---
section: Behaviour changes in all modes
---

- Rescue SSH with opkssh: the certificate principals no longer name the
  login user; `RESCUE_SSH_OPK_POLICY` alone decides, as in opkssh. A
  certificate from `opkssh login` (principal `opkssh-wildcard`) now logs in
  as `root`. A certificate without principals, from opkssh before the
  OpenSSH 10.3 fix, is refused, as golang.org/x/crypto v0.59.0 does (#973).
