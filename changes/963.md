---
section: Upgrade notes for CAPRF
---

- Disk encryption (`DISK_ENCRYPTION=luks2-tpm2`): phase 2 of the TPM2
  enrollment removes key slot 1 only after a boot whose initramfs opened the
  root with the TPM2 (#963). A boot that a passphrase or the recovery key
  opened keeps slot 1, posts the new status `tpm2-initrd-unlock-failed` to
  `DISK_ENCRYPTION_STATUS_URL` and tries again on the next boot.
  `DISK_ENCRYPTION_SKIP_INITRD_UNLOCK_CHECK=true` restores the old behaviour
  (transitional).
