---
section: Upgrade notes for CAPRF
---

- Disk encryption (`DISK_ENCRYPTION=luks2-tpm2`): when `cryptsetup luksDump`
  fails in phase 2 of the TPM2 enrollment, key slot 1 and the enrollment key
  now stay, the unit posts the new status `luks-dump-failed` to
  `DISK_ENCRYPTION_STATUS_URL` and tries again on the next boot (#967).
  Before, a failed `luksDump` before the slot 1 check counted as "slot 1 is
  gone" and the key was shredded.
