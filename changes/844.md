---
section: Behaviour changes in all modes
---

- GoBGP mode starts an unnumbered BGP session at once when the carrier of
  its uplink returns, like FRR. Before, GoBGP waited 1 to 5 s in Idle and
  refused the leaf's connection meanwhile (5 s when BFD went down before
  the carrier). BOOTy now deletes the peer and adds it again instead of
  `EnablePeer` (#844).
