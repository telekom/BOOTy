---
section: Behaviour changes in all modes
---

- GoBGP mode accepts the leaf's connection at once when the carrier of an
  unnumbered uplink returns, and connects itself after a random 1.5 to
  2 s, or at once with `BGP_CONNECT_AT_ONCE`. Before, GoBGP waited 1 to
  5 s in Idle and refused the leaf's connection meanwhile (5 s when BFD
  went down before the carrier). BOOTy now deletes the peer and adds it
  again instead of `EnablePeer`, except for a peer whose graceful restart
  stale routes GoBGP keeps (#844).
