---
section: Behaviour changes in all modes
---

- LLDP sending follows the carrier in every profile, like systemd-networkd
  249: a NIC without carrier gets no frames, and each carrier gain, also a
  short flap, starts the 4 fast frames 1 s apart again. Before, the default
  `networkd` profile kept sending into a link without carrier, so after the
  link came back the next frame went out only at the next 30 s tick. The
  sender checks the carrier every second in sysfs, so the first frame goes
  out within 1 s of the link coming up (#845).
