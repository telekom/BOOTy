---
section: Upgrade notes for CAPRF
---

- Disk encryption (`DISK_ENCRYPTION=luks2-tpm2-uki`): when `cryptsetup
  luksDump` fails in the first-boot enrollment, the unit now logs
  `luks-dump-failed`, fails and stays enabled, so the next boot tries again
  (#981). Before, a failed `luksDump` after the TPM2 enrollment skipped the
  wipe of key slot 1 and disabled the unit, which left the empty passphrase
  on the volume.
