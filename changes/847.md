---
section: Behaviour changes in all modes
---

- GoBGP mode uses the GoBGP fork tag v4.10.0-booty.18, which is upstream
  GoBGP master after 4.10.0 plus the fork patches (#847). After a session
  ends, GoBGP waits in Idle 1 s after a TCP close and 2 s after a
  NOTIFICATION, doubling up to 120 s while the sessions fail before
  Established, instead of a fixed 5 s. A peer without RFC 8950 extended
  next hop support still gets no IPv4 routes with an IPv6 next hop; this
  is upstream code now.
