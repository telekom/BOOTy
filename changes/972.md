---
section: Upgrade notes for CAPRF
---

- Disk encryption (`DISK_ENCRYPTION=luks2-tpm2-uki`,
  `SECUREBOOT_SIGNING=per-host`): the per-host kernel parameters that go
  into the signed UKI `.cmdline` are checked against an allow list of
  names (#972): `console`, `ds`, `ci.datasource`, `hugepages`,
  `hugepagesz`, `default_hugepagesz`, `isolcpus`, `nohz_full`, `rcu_nocbs`,
  `irqaffinity`, `iommu`, `intel_iommu`, `amd_iommu`, `net.ifnames`,
  `biosdevname`, `transparent_hugepage`, `numa_balancing`, and
  `mitigations=auto` or `mitigations=auto,nosmt`. The IOMMU names take
  only options that keep DMA protection on: `intel_iommu=off`,
  `amd_iommu=off`, `iommu=off`, `iommu=soft`, `intel_iommu=igfx_off` and
  the like are refused; `iommu=pt` and `intel_iommu=on` are allowed. The new
  `UKI_CMDLINE_POLICY` defaults to `warn`: another name is logged at WARN
  and listed in `secureBoot.uki.cmdlineNotAllowed` of the provisioning
  report. `enforce` refuses it before any key is made. **The default
  becomes `enforce` in the next minor release**: move the parameters that
  the reports list into the image `.cmdline`. The names refused before stay
  refused in both modes, and `mitigations` with any other value
  (`mitigations=off`), an IOMMU option that turns it off, the runlevels
  `2` to `5`, a cloud-init seed in `ds`/`ci.datasource`, `cc:`,
  `cloud-config-url` and `url` are now refused. A standby payload or deploy archive
  can not change `UKI_CMDLINE_POLICY`.
- `MACHINE_EXTRA_KERNEL_PARAMS` and `EXTRA_KERNEL_PARAMS` may contain
  commas (`isolcpus=1,3`, `mitigations=auto,nosmt`), in all modes (#972).
